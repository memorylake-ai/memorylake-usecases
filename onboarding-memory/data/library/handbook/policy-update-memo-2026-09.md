# Fernhill Robotics — People policy update, September 2026

_From: Dana Whitfield, Head of People, and Omar Reyes, Finance. Signed off 2026-09-01. Demo data — Fernhill Robotics is fictional._

## 1. Travel meal allowance (POL-MEAL v3)

Effective 2026-09-15 the travel meal allowance is **USD 60 per travel day**. Receipts are now required for any single meal above USD 25. This replaces the USD 65 allowance of April 2026.

## 2. Paid time off (POL-PTO v2)

Effective **2027-01-01**, paid vacation rises from 20 to **25 days per calendar year**, prorated in the first year. Until then the 20-day allowance in handbook §5.1 applies.
