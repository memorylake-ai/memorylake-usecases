# Fernhill Robotics — People policy update, June 2026

_From: Dana Whitfield, Head of People. Signed off 2026-06-12. Supersedes the matching sections of the Employee Handbook (January 2026 edition). Demo data — Fernhill Robotics is fictional._

## 1. Parental leave (POL-LEAVE v2)

Effective 2026-07-01, every parent — birth, adoptive or foster, primary or secondary caregiver — gets **20 weeks of fully paid leave**, available from the first day of employment. This replaces the 16-week primary / 4-week secondary split and the 6-month service requirement in handbook §4.2.

## 2. Travel meal allowance (POL-MEAL v2)

Recorded here for completeness: Finance (Omar Reyes) raised the travel meal allowance to **USD 65 per travel day**, effective 2026-04-01, signed off 2026-03-20. No receipts needed.

## What has not changed

Paid time off (handbook §5.1) and the home-office stipend (handbook §7.3) are unchanged.
