# State awards Calder 3.2 million dollars toward the seawall

The Tidewater Ledger, 2026-05-07. By Ruth Okafor, metro desk.

The state awarded Calder a 3.2 million dollar resilience grant for the Marsh Road seawall, less than the 5 million the town requested. The town will borrow the rest under the bond voters approved in March.
