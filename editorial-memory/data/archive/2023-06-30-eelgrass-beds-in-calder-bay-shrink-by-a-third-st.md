# Eelgrass beds in Calder Bay shrink by a third, state survey finds

The Tidewater Ledger, 2023-06-30. By Ruth Okafor, environment desk.

A state survey found eelgrass beds in Calder Bay covered about a third less area than in 2018. Biologists pointed to warmer summers and runoff from the Marsh Road culverts. The beds shelter young crab and flounder.
