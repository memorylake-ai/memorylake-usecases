# Calder High adds a robotics elective after a parent fundraiser

The Tidewater Ledger, 2023-04-02. By Priya Natarajan, schools desk.

Calder High will offer a robotics elective in the fall after parents raised 18,000 dollars at a spring auction. The class will hold 24 students. Teacher Owen Park said the waiting list was already longer than the class.
