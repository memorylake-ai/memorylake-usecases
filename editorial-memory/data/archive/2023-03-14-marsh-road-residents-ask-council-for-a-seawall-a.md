# Marsh Road residents ask council for a seawall after third flood this winter

The Tidewater Ledger, 2023-03-14. By Ruth Okafor, metro desk.

Water reached the porches on Marsh Road again on Sunday, the third time since December. About forty residents filled the council chamber to ask for a seawall along the Calder estuary. Councillor Dana Hale said a seawall would cost more than the town could borrow alone and promised a study. Residents said sandbags were no longer enough.
