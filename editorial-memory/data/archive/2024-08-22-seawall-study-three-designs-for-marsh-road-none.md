# Seawall study: three designs for Marsh Road, none cheap

The Tidewater Ledger, 2024-08-22. By Ruth Okafor, metro desk.

Coastline Engineering presented three seawall designs for Marsh Road. The cheapest, a rock revetment, would cost about 9 million dollars. A full concrete wall would cost 21 million. Residents asked whether the state would help pay.
