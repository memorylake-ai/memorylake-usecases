# Seawall budget vote: what Marsh Road residents need to know

Draft for The Tidewater Ledger. By Sam Achebe, metro desk (first week).

The council will vote in November on the construction budget for the Marsh Road seawall, which voters approved in March.

Supporters have long pointed to Harbor Authority tide gauge readings showing sea level at the harbor rising 9 millimeters a year, about twice the state average [source: 2025-03-06-tide-gauge-release.md]. The Authority's director said the data made the case "beyond doubt" [source: 2025-04-02-harbor-director-statement.md].

The budget combines bond proceeds with a state grant of $3.2 million [source: 2026-05-07-state-grant-award-letter.md]. Voters approved the bond 54% to 46% [source: 2026-03-12-bond-election-results.md].

Residents, business owners and fishermen slammed the slow pace of the project at an October meeting.
