# Master Services Agreement — Harbrook Logistics / Northwind Freight

Redline v2, returned by Northwind Freight counsel on 2025-03-10. Agreement ref. NWF-MSA-2025.
Clauses below are the negotiated text; unchanged boilerplate omitted.

- **2.1 Term.** Initial term of three years from 2025-04-01. Renewal requires the written agreement of both parties.
- **5.1 Payment.** Harbrook pays correct invoices within 30 days, less a 2% early-payment discount.
- **7.1 Indemnification.** Each party indemnifies the other against third-party intellectual-property infringement claims and gross negligence.
- **9.2 Limitation of liability.** Each party's total liability is capped at 18 months of fees paid under this Agreement.
- **9.3 Insurance.** Northwind maintains cargo liability insurance of at least USD 10,000,000 for the term.
- **11.1 Intellectual property.** Harbrook owns all route data, reports and deliverables produced under this Agreement.
