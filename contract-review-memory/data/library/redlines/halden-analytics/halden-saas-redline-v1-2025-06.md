# SaaS Subscription Agreement — Harbrook Logistics / Halden Analytics

Redline v1, returned by Halden Analytics counsel on 2025-06-02. Agreement ref. HAL-SAAS-2025.
Clauses below are the negotiated text; unchanged boilerplate omitted.

- **3.1 Term.** Subscription term of two years from 2025-07-01. Renewal requires a written order form.
- **6.1 Payment.** Harbrook pays correct invoices within 45 days.
- **8.1 Indemnification.** Each party indemnifies the other against third-party intellectual-property infringement claims and gross negligence.
- **10.1 Limitation of liability.** Each party's total liability is capped at 12 months of fees, except that claims arising from a personal-data breach are capped at 24 months of fees.
- **12.1 Intellectual property.** Halden keeps ownership of the Halden platform. Harbrook owns all Harbrook data and every report generated from it.
