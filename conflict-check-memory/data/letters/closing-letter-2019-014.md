# Closing letter - Matter 2019-014

Hollis & Reyes LLP
Date: 2020-06-18
From: Elena Hollis, Partner
To: Brightline Properties LLC, attention Ms. Rosa Varga, General Counsel

Dear Ms. Varga,

This letter confirms that our engagement to represent Brightline Properties LLC in the commercial lease dispute brought by its tenant Dovetail Bakery has concluded with the settlement signed on 2020-06-02.

Brightline Properties LLC is a former client of the firm. Our file for Matter 2019-014, including the lease, the correspondence with Dovetail Bakery's counsel and the settlement terms, will be retained for seven years.

Thank you for trusting us with this matter.

Elena Hollis
Partner, Hollis & Reyes LLP
