# Protocol CVT-118-201 - Dosing

Owner: Clinical pharmacology, Corvane Therapeutics. Version 3, effective 2026-09-01.

CVT-118 is given as an intravenous infusion once a week. The dose is 2 mg per kg of body weight, rounded to the nearest 10 mg, and never more than 160 mg per infusion.

Weigh the participant at every visit and recalculate the dose from that day's weight.
