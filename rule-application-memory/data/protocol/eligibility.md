# Protocol CVT-118-201 - Eligibility

Owner: Clinical operations, Corvane Therapeutics. Version 4, effective 2026-09-10.

Participants must be 18 to 75 years old (inclusive) on the day of screening. Kidney function: eGFR of at least 45 mL/min/1.73m2 at screening. Body weight between 50 kg and 120 kg.

A participant who fails screening may be rescreened once, no sooner than 28 days after the failed screen.
