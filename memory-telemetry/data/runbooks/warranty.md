# Warranty runbook - Bramblecrest Outfitters

Bramblecrest house-brand packs and tents carry a lifetime warranty against defects in materials and workmanship.

Zipper failures on house-brand gear are repaired free of charge at the Reno repair bench.

Damage from normal wear, accidents or misuse is repaired at cost, with a quote sent before any work starts.

Third-party brands are covered by their own manufacturer warranty, and the agent should share the maker's claim link.
