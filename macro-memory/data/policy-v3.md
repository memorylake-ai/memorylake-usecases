# Larkspur Gear Support Policy (v3, effective 2026-09-01)

Owner: Support Operations. This policy is the source of truth for every saved reply (macro) the support team sends.

## Refunds
Customers can request a full refund within 14 days of delivery. After 14 days we offer store credit only. Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5 business days.

## Shipping
Standard shipping is free on orders over 75 dollars. Orders under 75 dollars pay a flat 6 dollar shipping fee. Orders ship within 2 business days.

## Phone support
Phone support is available Monday to Friday, 8am to 6pm Central Time. There is no weekend phone support.

## Damaged items
If an item arrives damaged, send a free replacement. Trailhead Plus members may instead choose a full refund and keep the item.
