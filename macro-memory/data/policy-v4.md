# Larkspur Gear Support Policy (v4, effective 2026-11-01)

Owner: Support Operations. This policy is the source of truth for every saved reply (macro) the support team sends. It replaces v3.

## Refunds
Customers can request a full refund within 14 days of delivery. After 14 days we offer store credit only. Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5 business days.

## Shipping
Standard shipping is free on orders over 50 dollars. Orders under 50 dollars pay a flat 6 dollar shipping fee. Orders ship within 2 business days.

## Phone support
Phone support is available every day, 7am to 9pm Central Time, including weekends.

## Damaged items
If an item arrives damaged, send a free replacement. Trailhead Plus members may instead choose a full refund and keep the item.
