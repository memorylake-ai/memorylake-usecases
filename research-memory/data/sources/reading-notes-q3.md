# Reading notes — sodium-ion for grid storage (Q3 2026)

Mira Holt, Larkspur Research. Working notes, not for distribution.

## Halvorsen HBL-26-04 (lab study, May 2026)
- 4,200 cycles to 80% at a constant 25 °C, one cycle a day. Clean method, but **funded by Corvane Cells**
  and every cell came from Corvane → apply my 20% haircut on vendor-funded results.
- 61 USD/kWh pack cost for 2026 is plausible at 20 GWh a year; I use it in the cost model.
- Did not test partial state of charge or cold climates. That is exactly where grid sites live.

## Gridfield GF-2026-09 (field report, Aug 2026)
- 312 sites, 18 months, independent. 2,900 cycles projected; cold zone 2,300.
- Directly contradicts Halvorsen on cycle life. I trust the field data more for a grid model.
- Their point that lab numbers overstate lifetime throughput by ~30% is the key risk for our LCOS work.
