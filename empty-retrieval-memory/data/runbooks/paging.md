# Policy - Incident paging and escalation

Owner: SRE, Larkspur Pay. Last reviewed 2026-09-21.

A Sev1 incident pages the primary on-call within 5 minutes. If the primary has not acknowledged after 10 minutes, the secondary on-call is paged.

If a Sev1 is still open after 30 minutes, escalate to the Head of Platform, Rosa Lindqvist, by phone.
