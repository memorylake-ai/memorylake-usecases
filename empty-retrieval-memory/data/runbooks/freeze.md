# Policy - Holiday change freeze 2026

Owner: Engineering leadership, Larkspur Pay. Last reviewed 2026-10-05.

No production deploys from 26 November 2026 through 2 December 2026 (the Black Friday and Cyber Monday period). Hotfixes during the freeze need written approval from the VP of Engineering, Tomas Ibarra.

Rollbacks are allowed during the freeze without extra approval.
